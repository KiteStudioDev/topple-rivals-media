03 / The game

Aim. Crack. Topple.

Topple Rivals is a physics throwing game where three well-aimed hits send your rival tumbling from their platform. Drag to set your angle and power, then watch the platform crack, tilt, and finally give way.

Level up through changing scenery, adapt to wind and moving targets, and shift your own target position to change your throwing height. Use unlimited normal balls or unlock Bounce and Firework effects for new ways to land a hit.

Earn coins from victories to collect animal characters, target looks, and packs of special throws. Complete a duel each day to unlock a reward in a 30-day cycle. A short interactive practice helps you learn the controls.

How to play

  • Click or touch, drag to set direction and power, then release to throw.
  • Use the target-position button to change your target and throwing height.
  • In portrait mode, use View Target to inspect your rival before throwing.
  • Strike the opposing target three times to win and level up.

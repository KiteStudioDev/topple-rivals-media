03 / The game

Build. Dodge. Break.

Scrapline Rush: Core Breaker is a fast 3D assembly runner-shooter. Switch lanes to collect cannons, armor, legs, reactors, and shields, then use the machine you built to punch through the guardian waiting at the end of the line.

Every sector changes the required build and attack pattern. Read the warning lanes, dodge telegraphed strikes, wait for the weak point, and unload when the core opens. Chain enemy breaks to build momentum and earn more Scrap.

Face 50 guardians, spend Scrap on permanent upgrades, complete daily contracts, unlock timed energy weapons, and fill the Guardian Archive as the factory campaign expands.

How to play

  • Move with A/D or the Left/Right Arrow keys; drag left or right on touch screens.
  • Hold W, Up Arrow, J, the left mouse button, or touch to fire.
  • Use Space or right-click to unleash Core Salvo when fully charged.
  • Watch the red warning lanes, dodge the strike, then attack the exposed core.

Assembly decides the fight

Collect the correct modules before the guardian. An incomplete machine cannot break protected core armor.
